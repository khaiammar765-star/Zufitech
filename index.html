Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a repair take?

Most phone screen and battery jobs, plus common laptop fixes, are done the same day. Bigger jobs like motherboard repair or ordered parts may take 1–3 days — we'll always tell you the timing up front.

Do you charge for diagnosis?

No. We check the problem for free and give you a clear quote before we start. You only pay if you approve the repair.

Is there a warranty?

Yes — every repair comes with a warranty. If the same issue comes back within the warranty period, we fix it again at no extra charge.

Which brands and devices do you handle?

All major brands and models — desktops, custom PCs, laptops, Android phones, iPhones, iPads and tablets. If you're not sure, just WhatsApp us a photo.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours — Mon–Thu 7:00pm–11:30pm, Fri 8:00pm–12:00am, Sat–Sun 12:00pm–1:00am. For a faster turnaround, WhatsApp us first so we can prepare.

Got a Device That Needs Fixing?

WhatsApp us a photo of the problem and we'll send you an estimate.
