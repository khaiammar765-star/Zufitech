About ZufiTech

Your friendly neighbourhood tech shop in Nilai — repairs and sales you can trust.

ZufiTech is a one-stop computer and phone shop based in Taman Desa Indah, Nilai. We repair and sell desktops, custom PCs, laptops, phones, iPads and tablets — all under one roof.

We started with a simple idea: tech repair should be honest, fast and fair. That means a free diagnosis, a clear quote before we touch anything, quality parts, and a warranty that stands behind our work. No jargon, no surprise charges.

Why customers choose us

Locals keep coming back because we treat every device like our own. Whether it's a cracked phone screen on a busy morning or a full PC build for gaming and work, we take the time to get it right — and to explain what we did and why.

A registered local business

ZufiTech System Enterprise is a registered business under SSM (202603273361 / CT0170453-T), led hands-on by founder Khairul Ammar bin Abd Rahmat. You deal directly with the person doing the work — real expertise and a genuinely personal level of service.

Right here in Nilai

Drop by the shop during opening hours, or WhatsApp us a photo of the problem for a quick estimate. We're proud to serve Nilai and the surrounding Negeri Sembilan area.

Honest & Transparent

Free diagnosis and a clear quote first. You decide before any work begins.

Quality Parts

Warranty Backed
