About Us

Your Local Tech Shop in Nilai

Honest repairs, fair prices, and friendly service for the people of Nilai and beyond.

ZufiTech is a computer and phone repair and sales shop based in Taman Desa Indah, Nilai, Negeri Sembilan. We help students, families, gamers and businesses keep their devices running — and find the right one when it's time to upgrade.

Whether it's a cracked phone screen, a laptop that won't turn on, a slow desktop, or a tablet that needs a new battery, we diagnose the real problem first and tell you honestly whether it's worth repairing. No pressure, no hidden charges.

What We Do

We repair desktops, PCs, laptops, handphones, iPads and tablets of all brands. We also sell new and quality pre-owned devices, and build custom gaming, workstation and server PCs to any budget.

Why People Trust Us

Every repair comes with a warranty, every quote is given up front, and most phone and laptop jobs are done the same day. We treat your device the way we'd treat our own — carefully and properly.
